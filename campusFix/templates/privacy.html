Privacy & Anonymity

CampusFix is built with anonymity by design. Reporters are represented using system-generated identifiers rather than personal details.

Administrators cannot see the identity of reporters. Only a designated super administrator can access identity mappings for security and audit purposes.

Report data is used strictly for issue resolution and system improvement. No personal information is shared, sold, or publicly displayed.

Your voice matters — and it remains protected.
