About CampusFix

CampusFix is a web-based platform designed to help students and staff report issues and challenges affecting campus life and nearby living spaces.

From broken facilities and connectivity issues to safety and living-condition concerns, CampusFix ensures that every report is tracked, addressed, and resolved.

The system is built with anonymity at its core, allowing users to speak up without fear while enabling administrators to take meaningful action.

Developed as part of the KYU Innovation Summit.
