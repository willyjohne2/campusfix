Help & How It Works

  • Register or log in to access reporting features.
  • Click Report Issue and provide accurate details.
  • Your identity remains hidden through system-generated identifiers.
  • Track the status of your report in real time.
  • Receive updates when the issue status changes.

After submitting a report, you can comment on your own report to provide updates or additional details. Administrators may also comment to request clarification or confirm resolution.

To prevent misuse, comments are restricted and not open to the public.

Only authorized administrators can manage reported issues. If an issue requires urgent attention, submit it with clear and accurate information.
